About NorthStar

NorthStar Trading Card Collection & Deck Manager is designed for Magic: The Gathering players and collectors who want one place to organize their cards, browse card information, and manage their decks.

Project Goals

The goal of NorthStar is to make managing a growing card collection easier by connecting collection tracking, card information, deck management, and wishlists in one website.

Future development may include features such as card recommendations, collection and deck analytics, price tracking, and other tools that help players manage their cards and decks.
