My Collection

Keep track of the Magic: The Gathering cards in your personal collection. NorthStar will help organize your cards and make it easier to see what you own and where your cards are being used.

Collection Details

Cards in your collection can include information such as card name, set, type, color, rarity, condition, legality, and quantity.

NorthStar will also help track whether a card is available in your collection or currently being used in one of your decks.
