My Collection

Keep track of the Magic: The Gathering cards in your personal collection. NorthStar helps organize your cards and makes it easier to see what you own, how many copies you have, and where your cards are being used.

Collection Details

Each card can be organized by important details such as card name, set, type, color, rarity, condition, and quantity.

Collection Tracking

NorthStar is designed to show whether cards are available in your collection or currently being used in a deck. This makes it easier to manage a growing collection without having to search through cards manually.
