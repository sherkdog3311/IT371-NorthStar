Deck Manager

The NorthStar Deck Manager is designed to help Magic: The Gathering players organize their decks and keep track of which cards from their collection are currently being used.

Decks can be organized by format, colors, strategy, and other information that makes managing multiple decks easier.

Deck Features

Deck Lists

Keep an organized list of the cards included in each deck and track the number of copies being used.

Format

Identify the format of each deck, such as Commander, Modern, Standard, or other Magic formats.

Deck Strategy

Record the main strategy or theme of a deck to make it easier to understand how the deck is intended to play.

Collection Link

Connect deck lists with the Collection section so users can see which owned cards are currently being used.

Example Decks

A Commander deck could be organized by its commander, color identity, strategy, and cards currently assigned from the collection. For example, an Aesi deck could be identified as a green and blue landfall deck focused on playing additional lands, creating value from landfall abilities, and building toward powerful creatures.

Keeping this information connected to the collection helps a player quickly determine where important cards are being used before making changes to another deck.
