My Decks

Build and organize Magic: The Gathering decks using cards from your NorthStar collection. Decks can be created for formats such as Commander, Standard, and Modern.

Deck Management

NorthStar will connect deck lists directly with your card collection, making it easier to see which cards are currently being used in a deck and which cards are still available.

This will help players organize multiple decks without losing track of where cards from their collection are being used.
