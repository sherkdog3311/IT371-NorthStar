Card Browser

The NorthStar Card Browser is designed to make it easier to search for Magic: The Gathering cards and view useful card information in one place. Players can use this page to find cards for their collection or research possible additions to their decks.

Card Information

Card Name

Search and identify cards by name to quickly locate specific cards in the NorthStar database.

Card Type

View whether a card is a creature, artifact, enchantment, instant, sorcery, planeswalker, or land.

Color

Organize cards by color identity to help locate cards that may work with a particular deck.

Rarity

View card rarity information such as common, uncommon, rare, and mythic rare.

Search and Browse

NorthStar is designed to allow users to browse cards and narrow their search using important card details. Search tools can help players locate cards by name, type, color, set, or rarity instead of searching through an entire collection manually.

Card information can also be used alongside the Collection and Decks sections of NorthStar, making it easier to determine whether a card is already owned or being used in an existing deck.
