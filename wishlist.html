My Wishlist

Keep track of Magic: The Gathering cards you want to add to your collection. The Wishlist will provide one place to save cards you may want to purchase, trade for, or use in a future deck.

Planning Future Additions

Wishlist cards can be compared with your current collection to help avoid purchasing cards you already own and make it easier to plan future additions to your decks.

As NorthStar develops, the Wishlist can eventually work with other features such as card recommendations, price tracking, and deck management.
