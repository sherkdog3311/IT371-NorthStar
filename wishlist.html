My Wishlist

The NorthStar Wishlist provides a place to keep track of cards that a player wants to add to their collection. Instead of keeping separate notes or trying to remember individual cards, wanted cards can be organized in one location.

Wishlist Features

Wanted Cards

Keep a list of cards that you are interested in adding to your personal collection.

Deck Upgrades

Identify cards that are being considered as upgrades or additions to existing decks.

Collection Goals

Track cards wanted for personal collections, favorite card types, sets, or other collecting goals.

Future Purchases

Organize possible future purchases before deciding which cards should be added to the collection.

Using the Wishlist

Cards saved to the Wishlist can eventually be moved into the Collection after they are acquired. This keeps NorthStar organized while providing a clear separation between cards that are already owned and cards that are still being sought.

Wishlist cards may also be connected to future deck ideas, allowing players to keep track of cards they want before making changes to an existing deck or beginning a new one.
